Read bills in Congress in plain English, vote Yea, Nay or Abstain, then see how other people and Congress voted.

Billboard is an independent app and is not affiliated with Congress or any government agency. Bill information comes from Congress.gov and GovInfo, roll-call votes from Congress.gov and the U.S. Senate, and In the news picks are chosen with AI from public news headlines.

Download on the App Store

For iPhone. In the App Store, Billboard is listed as BillboardProject.

Neutral and private

  • Billboard takes no side on any bill. It has no ads, no paid placement, and no sponsored polls, and we do not sell personal information.
  • Other people never see how you voted. Results show only combined counts, and a state or district breakdown appears only after enough people there have voted.
  • No trackers.

In the app

Billboard turns bills in Congress into plain English and lets you say where you stand.

Billboard is built for everyday people, not policy experts.

Read

Every bill gets a plain-language summary of what it does and why it matters. Summaries are written with AI from the official bill text and labelled that way, and you can report a problem from any summary.

Vote

Vote Yea, Nay or Abstain on any bill and add your reasons if you like. Skipping a bill never counts as a vote.

See results

After you vote, see how everyone voted, how people with a verified district voted, and how Congress voted. Lists never rank bills by how many votes they have.

Follow what matters to you

Follow topics, life areas such as taxes, housing and student loans, and members of Congress. Watch a bill to hear when it moves, and catch up with the Weekly Brief.

Know your Congress

Find your district from your address, see your senators and representative, and follow any member.

Track every outcome

Each bill shows where it stands: new, updated, passed, failed or became law.
