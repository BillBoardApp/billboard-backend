Billboard Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Billboard collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Billboard mobile app and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Billboard, we may collect:

  • Account information, including your email address used for authentication (AWS Cognito). We also keep salted one-way hashes (irreversibly scrambled forms) of your email address, both as you entered it and with any "+" tag removed (and, for Gmail addresses, any dots), used only to stop duplicate accounts.
  • Optional profile and preference information, such as saved preferences, followed topics, and represented districts.
  • District verification information you submit, including state/district selection and, if you choose address verification, address fields such as street, city, state, and ZIP code. We do not store the street address itself; we store the state and congressional district it resolves to. If a lookup fails, our operational logs record the state, ZIP code, and one-way hashed (irreversibly scrambled) forms of the other address fields for troubleshooting; those logs are kept for 30 days. If you verify by address, we also keep salted one-way hashes of the address as the Census Bureau matched it, with and without any unit number, and the dates of your last three address changes. We use them only to limit how many accounts can verify at one address and how often an account can change its verified address.
  • Network information when you verify your district: a one-way hash (an irreversibly scrambled form) of your device's IP address. We do not store the IP address itself, and we do not look up where it is located. We keep the hash only to investigate abuse of district verification.
  • Device and notification information, including device identifier, push token, and notification settings.
  • App integrity information. To keep votes and accounts genuine, the app uses Apple's App Attest and DeviceCheck to show it is a real copy of Billboard on a real Apple device. We store a random identifier for your app installation, its public key, Apple's attestation receipt and a use counter, which accounts have used that installation, and with each vote the result of this check. Apple keeps a small per-device counter of accounts created, with no account information. The installation records expire about 400 days after their last use, and deleting your account deletes its link to the installation.
  • In-app activity records required to provide app features, including feedback submissions and preference updates.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, maintain, and secure your account and app features.
  • Personalize bill feeds, notifications, and related content.
  • Process and store your feedback and preference settings.
  • Verify district data when you request district verification.
  • Detect abuse, prevent fraud, troubleshoot issues, and improve service reliability.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers who operate app infrastructure on our behalf (for example AWS services used for authentication, storage, messaging, and push delivery), and when required by law or valid legal process.

When you choose address verification, the address you enter is sent to the United States Census Bureau's public geocoding service to determine your congressional district. The Census Bureau's handling of that request is governed by its own policies. In some configurations we may also send your device's IP address (and nothing else) to a third-party IP geolocation provider to estimate the state it is located in; this lookup is currently disabled.

Bill summaries shown in the app are generated by an AI model hosted on AWS from public legislative text. No personal information is sent to that model.

4. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods may vary by data type and operational need.

We keep short-lived backups so we can recover from outages and mistakes. Our databases can be restored to any point in the previous 35 days. Each night we also back up the sign-in directory: your email address, account identifier, state, and district (not your political party, other profile details, or password). Each of those copies is deleted about two weeks after it is made. When you delete your account, your data is removed from the live service right away, and its copies in these backups are deleted as the backups expire, within about five weeks. If we ever restore from a backup, we do not restore accounts that were deleted after that backup was taken.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can update profile and preference information in the app.
  • You can manage push permissions through your device settings.
  • You can delete your account and its data from within the app (Profile → Settings → Delete account and data; in version 1.1 and earlier, Profile → Delete Account And Data). Deletion removes your votes, preferences, device registrations, digests, and verification record, including the email hashes described above and your account's links to the address hashes and to your app installation.
  • You may also request account or data assistance by contacting support.

7. Children’s Privacy

Billboard is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and may provide additional notice in the app or service.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, account help, or data requests, contact: contact@billboard-project.com
