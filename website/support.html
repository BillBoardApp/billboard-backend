Contact

Email contact@billboard-project.com. Say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead. If it is about a bill, give its number, such as H.R. 1234. It also helps to give the app version, which is in Profile → Settings → About Billboard.

In the app, Contact us in About Billboard opens an email to the same address.

Delete your account

In the app, open Profile → Settings → Delete account and data, type DELETE, and tap Delete account and data. In version 1.1 and earlier, it is called Delete Account And Data.

Deleting your account removes your Billboard data. This can't be undone. The privacy policy says what deletion removes and when its backups expire.

If you no longer have the app, email contact@billboard-project.com from the address you sign in with, with “Delete my account” in the subject. We reply to that address to confirm before we delete anything, and again when your account is deleted.

Your data

What Billboard keeps, why and for how long, and your choices, are in the privacy policy, under Your Choices and Rights.

Signing in

Billboard emails you a code when you create an account and when you reset your password. The email comes from Billboard <no-reply@billboard-project.com>, with the subject “Your Billboard verification code”.

  • If it hasn't arrived after a few minutes, check your junk or spam folder.
  • To get a new code when you create an account, tap Resend code.
  • For a password reset, tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen and send the code again.

Where you vote

Billboard finds your congressional district from your address with the U.S. Census Bureau's public geocoder. The five U.S. territories are included. We send your address to the U.S. Census Bureau to find your district. We don't keep your street address on our servers.

To change it, open Profile and tap Change next to Where you vote. If the district looks wrong, check the address and try again, or choose your district yourself. If you choose your district yourself, your votes count in national results but not in state or district results. If it still looks wrong, email us the state and district you expected. You don't need to send your address.

The 120th Congress begins on January 3, 2027, with new House maps in some states. From that day, open Profile and check where you vote.

Report a problem with a summary

Bill summaries are written with AI from the official text, and the AI can make mistakes. Every summary in the app, and on shared bill pages, has a Report a problem link. It opens an email to contact@billboard-project.com that names the bill. Say what looks wrong. We check the summary against the official bill text, and correct it if it is wrong.

Results

Results open after you vote Yea, Nay or Abstain, so other people's votes don't sway yours. A bill's results open once it has 5 votes.

Other people never see how you voted. Results show only combined counts, and a state or district breakdown appears only after enough people there have voted.

Accessibility

Dark mode, the largest text sizes and VoiceOver are supported throughout. To choose light or dark, open Profile → Settings → App settings → Appearance.

If something in the app or on this website is hard to use with VoiceOver, a large text size or another setting, please email us and say where.
