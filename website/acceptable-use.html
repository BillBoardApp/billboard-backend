Acceptable Use

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Billboard is open source. You can build the app from our source code and use it with our service, the same service the App Store app uses. These rules keep votes genuine and the service working for everyone. If you publish a build for other people, please run your own backend, as our contributing guide asks.

If you use the project's hosted service from anything other than the project's App Store or TestFlight builds (a build you made, or your own code):

  • Use your own account and vote as yourself. Do not create accounts in bulk or with throwaway email addresses.
  • Keep test votes few. Votes count toward results other people see.
  • No scripts or bots. Do not script sign-ups, votes or reads, scrape the service or the share site, or load-test it. For bill data in bulk, use Congress.gov and GovInfo.
  • Respect the limits and checks. Do not try to get around the service's limits, sign-up checks or app integrity check. If you find a way, report it privately as SECURITY.md describes, and do not use it.
  • Leave other people alone. Do not try to see how anyone else voted or get into other accounts, and do not enter anyone else's email address or street address.

The project may limit, suspend or delete accounts, votes or builds that break these rules. It may change or stop the service at any time, and builds from older or changed code may stop working.

Our privacy policy applies when you use our service this way. Its section 8 explains what that means for a build you sign yourself.

Questions: contact@billboard-project.com
